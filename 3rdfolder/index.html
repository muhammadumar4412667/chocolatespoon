Trust basics

Pause. Check. Then decide.

Scammers often rely on speed and emotion. These three habits can give you a moment to verify what you are seeing.

01 / SLOW DOWN

Pressure is a signal.

Urgent countdowns, threats, surprise prizes and “act now” demands deserve a second look.

02 / VERIFY

Check the source.

Open the official service yourself instead of trusting a link, caller or message that asks you to act.

03 / PROTECT

Keep control.

Never share passwords, one-time codes or payment details just because a stranger creates urgency.
